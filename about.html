// who is josh

A designer who ships his own code

I got into this by accident — fixing a friend's broken website turned into freelancing, which turned into a full design-and-build practice. I care about the same thing on every project: does this actually make someone's day a little easier to get through. Everything else — the tool, the framework, the trend — is negotiable.

Outside of client work I spend a lot of time refining JoshTech's own systems: components, templates and small tools that let me move fast without cutting corners.
